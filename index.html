Case study · Organic search / SEO

Client: thesearchingsouls.com — trekking & travel content site
The site was invisible for the exact searches its readers were already typing — specific Himalayan treks like Dayara Bugyal and Brahmatal, not generic travel terms. Over 12 months I rebuilt the site's technical SEO foundation and content structure around these long-tail queries, then kept optimizing month over month as the data came in. The result is a steady, compounding climb in both impressions and clicks, visible directly in Search Console.

Case study · Meta ads / Lead generation

20–30 leads a day, every day, on ₹600–700 in spend

Client: Tour & travel operator — Meta lead ads
This client needed a steady flow of qualified leads without ballooning ad spend. I built and ran Meta lead ad campaigns, tested audiences and creative until cost per lead stabilized, and scaled the daily budget gradually rather than all at once. The campaign now runs on a modest ₹600–700 daily budget and consistently delivers 20–30 leads a day, with output continuing to grow as the account matures.
