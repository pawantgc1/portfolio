Case Study 01 · Organic Search / SEO

thesearchingsouls.com — 183K clicks from long-tail trek SEO

Client Overview

A trekking & travel content site targeting Himalayan trek routes (Dayara Bugyal, Brahmatal, and similar), competing against larger, better-known travel publishers.

Challenge

The site was invisible for the exact long-tail searches its future readers were already typing, and had no structured technical SEO foundation to build on.

Strategy

Prioritize long-tail, high-intent trek queries over generic travel terms; fix technical SEO issues first; restructure content around search intent.

Implementation

Ran a full technical SEO audit and fixes, rebuilt on-page structure around target keywords, and monitored Search Console weekly to guide ongoing content and internal linking decisions over 12 months.

Results

Steady, compounding month-over-month growth in both impressions and clicks, with several trek-specific queries now ranking on page one.

Case Study 02 · Meta Ads / Lead Generation

Tour Operator — 20–30 daily leads on ₹600–700 spend

Client Overview

A tour & travel operator needing a predictable, affordable flow of qualified leads through Meta lead ads.

Challenge

No consistent lead flow, and a limited daily budget that needed to work efficiently from day one rather than "spend big and hope."

Strategy

Start lean, test audiences and creatives quickly, and scale budget only once cost per lead was stable and predictable.

Implementation

Built Meta lead ad campaigns, ran structured creative and audience tests, and increased the daily budget gradually as performance data came in.

Results

A consistent 20–30 leads per day on a ₹600–700 daily budget, with lead volume continuing to grow as the account matures.
